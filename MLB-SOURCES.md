# MLB 2026 roster sources

This is a fixed 2026 snapshot. Offseason transactions do not replace the requested end-of-season teams.

## Scope

Non-playoff teams use the active 28-man roster on their latest date with exactly 28 active players at or before the final regular-season game (excluding temporary 29th-player expansion). Playoff teams use the active 26-man roster on the latest available 26-player game date in the furthest round reached. Documented injured regulars are additional inclusions, never described as official active/postseason roster members. Injury candidates must appear on that team's historical injured list and have meaningful 2026 or 2025 major-league playing time. Jordan Westburg is explicitly included.

## Position pools

Highest-2026-PA primary-position regular per team (150 PA; catchers 100 PA), plus documented injured regulars.

100 innings and 15 starts; up to 75, including protected injured regulars.

40 innings or 10 saves; up to 75, including protected injured regulars. Usage order: outs plus six per save.

Most 2026 starts by position; 2025 starts then official roster position for injured players with no 2026 position starts. Pitchers use starting/relief appearance majority. Shohei Ohtani is the sole exception and appears in both DH and SP. Team cards retain multiple played positions.

Only Shohei Ohtani appears in more than one position pool (DH and SP). Team cards show multiple played positions. Complete per-player position starts, playing-time inputs, injury statuses and roster membership are in [the audit](assets/mlb/roster-audit.json).

## Imagery

Portraits and logos come from official MLB static assets, identified by MLB person/team ID. Copies are hosted with this site for reliable image exports. Source URLs, hashes and capture dates accompany each headshot. MLB's current headshot endpoint is used because its year-specific endpoint was unavailable; capture freezes the image used at publication but cannot guarantee the cap shown matches every historical roster assignment. Failed or generic images use jersey fallbacks. Public availability does not grant an open redistribution license; MLB and relevant rights holders retain their rights.

## Team-card secondary positions

Primary position plus substantial secondary roles. Infield: 20 starts and 15% of position starts, or 10 current starts plus 25 prior-season starts. Outfield: 25 starts and 25% of outfield starts. DH: 25 starts and 25% of position starts. Injured players without current starts use established prior-season roles. Ohtani is the sole two-way exception.

## Injured pitcher pools

Protected injured starters: 15 starts in 2026 or 20 in 2025, plus documented notable aces Hunter Greene, Corbin Burnes, Pablo López, Spencer Schwellenbach and Shane Bieber. Protected injured relievers: 45 games or 5 saves in 2026, plus established high-profile bullpen arms. Remaining places use 2026 usage. Both pitching pools cap at 75.
