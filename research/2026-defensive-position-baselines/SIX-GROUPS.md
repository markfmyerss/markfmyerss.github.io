## Classification policy

Exactly one group per defender. Preserve original DL/LB/DB parent. Prefer precise official roster labels; use ESPN roster/depth roles for broad labels. DE and EDGE are separate listed-role buckets, not disjoint football skills. Conflicting or missing roles remain for review.

- Precise official roster designations such as DT, NT, DE, EDGE, ILB, CB, and S take priority.
- Broad DL/LB/DB entries use ESPN roster positions or base-defense depth-chart slots.
- ILB includes other off-ball linebackers; four-man-front outside linebackers are not automatically treated as pass rushers.
- DE and EDGE overlap as football concepts. Here they are separate listed-role buckets: defensive-line ends versus outside rush linebackers/explicit EDGE players. A player appears once.
- A formation slot can differ from the roster position: Madubuike is listed DT on ESPN's roster but LDE in its base 3-4 chart. His DT designation puts him in IDL.
- Eligibility counts remain provisional because the original unmatched snap identities still need review. No missing record was assigned zero snaps.
